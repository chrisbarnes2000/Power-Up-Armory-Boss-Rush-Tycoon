## 📜 Historical Record
Plunged the northern territories into a century of bone-winter before champions wielding the Phoenix Feather ended the curse.

---

## 👁️ Lore & Biology
A fallen necromancer king who achieved eternal undeath through dark soul bindings. Surrounds himself with bone frost.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Behavior & Enrage Mechanics
Upon physical death, his phylactery instantaneously triggers, resurrecting his skeletal form at full combat potency.

---

## 🎯 Tactical Weakness
Phylactery resonance can be exhausted. Champions who survive his second phase can permanently shatter his crown.
