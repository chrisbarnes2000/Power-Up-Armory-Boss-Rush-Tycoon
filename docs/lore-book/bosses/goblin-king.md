## 📜 Historical Record
Over 12,000 recorded skirmishes on the outer rim. Primary source of early gold seeds.

---

## 👁️ Lore & Biology
Ruler of the lower subterranean warrens. Surrounds himself with screeching minions who throw themselves into weapon swings.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Behavior & Enrage Mechanics
Attacks in rapid flurries and relies on sheer numbers to wear down solitary adventurers before looting their armor.

---

## 🎯 Tactical Weakness
Susceptible to precision burst damage. Eliminating him quickly scatters his minions into disarray.

---
