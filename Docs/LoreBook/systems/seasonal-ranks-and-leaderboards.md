## 🌌 The Cycle of Seasons & Eternal Ascension

In the cosmos of the **Power-Up Armory**, power is not static. It ebbs, surges, and coalesces in cyclical epochs known as **Seasonal Horizons**. 

Every calendar month, the celestial convergence resets the **Monthly Arena**, opening the gates for rising champions to challenge the 8 World Bosses, test new armory loadouts, and claim royal bounties. At the end of each lunar cycle, top contenders are immortalized in the **Hall of Champions**, while the **Annual Grand Championship** chronicles the eternal sovereign legends who shape the realm across entire years.

## 👹 Boss Executioner & Undying Challenger Bounties

Each of the **8 World Bosses** maintains an active bounty ledger tracking the two extremes of combat prowess:

1. **👑 Top Executioner (Most Kills)**:
   - The warrior who has delivered the most fatal blows to that specific boss.
   - Rewards an ongoing `+15,000 Gold` prestige bounty and top billing in the boss dossier.

2. **💀 Undying Challenger (Most Deaths / Perseverance)**:
   - The gladiator who has fallen the most times attempting to overcome that boss's brutal mechanics.
   - Honors resilience over surrender, proving that true champions are forged through perseverance.

## 📊 Live Telemetry & Verification Matrix

All combat stats are captured in deterministic real-time registers:
- **Max Single-Hit Damage**: Highest single strike damage recorded across all arena runs.
- **Evasion Counter**: Successful speed-based dodge calculations.
- **Special Invocation Counter**: Artifact procs (Supernovas, Void Rifts, Dragonfire bursts).
- **Kill/Death Ledger**: Comprehensive ledger tracking personal win/loss ratios against every threat level.
- **Cloud Verification**: Cryptographically synchronized with Firestore to guarantee leaderboard integrity.
